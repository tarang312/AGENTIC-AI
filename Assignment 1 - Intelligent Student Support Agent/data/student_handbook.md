# PGDM (Big Data Analytics / Artificial Intelligence & Data Science)
## Student Handbook & Academic Policy Guide (Batch 2025-2027)

---

### Section 1: Academic Programs & Specialization Guidelines
1.1 **Core Curriculum**: Students enrolled in the PGDM (BDA / AI & DS) must complete 108 credits across 6 trimesters/semesters. 
1.2 **Specialization Tracks**: Students select their major specialization at the conclusion of Term 2. Current available tracks:
- **Artificial Intelligence & Data Science**: Focuses on Natural Language Processing (NLP), Deep Learning, Computer Vision, Reinforcement Learning, and Agentic Systems. Recommended electives include Advanced NLP, Generative AI Systems, and Distributed Computing.
- **Big Data Analytics**: Focuses on Cloud Data Warehousing, Apache Spark, Large Scale Data Pipelines, and MLOps.
- **Financial Analytics & FinTech**: Focuses on Algorithmic Trading, Risk Modeling, and Blockchain.
1.3 **Course Drop/Add Policy**: Students may add or drop an elective course within 10 calendar days of the trimester start date with approval from the Academic Programme Office. After 10 days, course withdrawals result in a 'W' grade on the transcript.

---

### Section 2: Tuition Fees, Installments & Payment Deadlines
2.1 **Fee Structure**: Total programme tuition is ₹12,00,000 payable across 4 equal installments of ₹3,00,000 at the beginning of each semester/term.
2.2 **Standard Due Dates**:
- Trimester 1 / Installment 1: July 15th
- Trimester 2 / Installment 2: November 15th
- Trimester 3 / Installment 3: March 15th
- Trimester 4 / Installment 4: July 15th
2.3 **Late Payment Penalty**: A late payment surcharge of ₹250 per calendar day is applied starting from the day following the deadline. If fees remain unpaid for more than 15 days past the due date, access to the Learning Management System (LMS) and examination portal is temporarily suspended.

---

### Section 3: Payment Portal Technical Failures & Grace Period Policy
3.1 **Portal Failure Clause**: If the online payment gateway experiences a technical failure, transaction timeout, or server downtime on the official due date, students are granted an **automatic technical grace period of two (2) working days** without incurring late penalty charges.
3.2 **Next Working Day Payment**: A student encountering a gateway debit failure or portal error on the deadline is permitted to complete the fee payment on the next working day.
3.3 **Required Action for Verification**: To validate the technical failure waiver, the student must notify the Accounts Office (accounts@institute.edu) within 24 hours of the failure, providing:
- Screenshot of the error screen or bank deduction SMS/email.
- Bank Unique Transaction Reference (UTR) or reference number.
3.4 **Reconciliation Period**: Bank-to-institute settlement reconciliation takes up to 48 hours. If money has been debited from the student's account, it will automatically reflect or refund within 3 to 5 business days.

---

### Section 4: Scholarships & Merit-cum-Means Financial Aid
4.1 **Merit Scholarships**: Awarded to the top 10% of admitted candidates based on entrance test scores and undergraduate GPA (typically 25% to 50% tuition waiver).
4.2 **Renewal Criteria**: To maintain scholarship eligibility across subsequent terms:
- The recipient must maintain a minimum Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) of **3.50 on a 4.00 scale** (or 8.0 on a 10.0 scale).
- The student must maintain at least **80% overall physical attendance** across all enrolled courses.
- The student must have zero disciplinary infractions or academic integrity violations.
4.3 **Probation**: If a student's term CGPA falls between 3.20 and 3.49, the scholarship is placed on provisional probation for one term. If the score is not raised in the subsequent term, the scholarship is permanently revoked.

---

### Section 5: Attendance Rules & Leave of Absence
5.1 **Mandatory Minimum Attendance**: 80% physical attendance is mandatory in each registered course to be eligible to sit for end-term examinations.
5.2 **Medical Leave**: Absence due to medical illness up to 10% can be condoned by submitting a registered medical practitioner's certificate to the Student Affairs Office within 5 working days of resuming classes.
5.3 **Shortage Consequences**: Attendance below 70% in any course leads to immediate deregistration from that course, requiring the student to re-take the course in the subsequent academic cycle.

---

### Section 6: IT Facilities & Campus Code of Conduct
6.1 **Student Credentials & Security**: Student portal passwords, single-sign-on (SSO) credentials, and Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) tokens are strictly confidential. Sharing login credentials with other students or third parties is a severe disciplinary violation. Support staff and automated agents will never ask for or store student passwords.
6.2 **Campus Regulations**: Standard academic decorum must be observed across all lecture halls, laboratories, and common spaces. 
6.3 **Unspecified Regulations / Gaps**: Any special campus requests, off-campus housing permissions, or novel facility usage not explicitly documented in this handbook must be submitted via an official Support Ticket to the Dean of Student Affairs for individual committee review.
